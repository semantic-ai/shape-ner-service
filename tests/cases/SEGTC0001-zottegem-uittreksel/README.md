## SEGTC0001-zottegem-uittreksel

**Title**: "uittreksel with one decision of six articles, also harvested from the HTML publication"

**Description**: "An uittreksel holds one decision: the expression itself is enriched as the besluit:Besluit (motivation,
content) and linked to its six articles with eli:has_part. Sub-articles (1.1, 2.1, ...) are part of their article.
The same decision is in the triplestore from the HTML harvest with the same title: an oa:Annotation (oa:linking) links
the expression to it, and its title and description are not repeated on the expression. The original itself is not
changed."

**Source**: Uittreksels_22-06-2026_70589 of the Zottegem municipal council of 22 June 2026, point 17
(https://lblod.zottegem.be/LBLODWeb/Home/Overzicht/e3d9480acc0cf400e11ab7afe2bcea144038dd2ef71d77247d5159c7ce8efb1a/GetPublication/?filename=Uittreksels_22-06-2026_70589.pdf)

**Input**
- `input.ttl`: expression <http://data.lblod.info/id/expressions/2b60747f-c8f7-4311-bbbd-4623c055075e> with its full text
- `context.ttl`: the original <https://lblod.zottegem.be/LBLODWeb/id/besluiten/50af1c5e78a1407699733aaf1df121d2-70589>
